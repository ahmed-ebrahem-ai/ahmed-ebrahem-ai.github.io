How I work

Ask first

I go into the department and ask what the work actually is. The requirement someone writes down and the problem they have are rarely the same thing.

Find the loop

I look for the handful of steps a person repeats every week. That is where automation pays, and it is usually not where people expect it.

Hand it over

An agent that finds jobs, scores them, and writes the application

Personal project — built for four friends n8nClaude APIClaude CodeGoogle SheetsMessaging

Problem
Four friends were each losing hours a week to the same loop: search boards, read postings, guess whether it was worth applying, rewrite the same CV again.
Approach
I sat down with each of them separately and wrote down what they were actually looking for. That turned into four scoring profiles rather than one generic filter — the hard part was never the plumbing, it was defining what "a good fit" means for a specific person.
Build
An LLM extracts the real requirements from each posting. A scoring step weighs them against the person's profile and subtracts points for signals that mean wrong job family or wrong seniority — MLOps stacks, infrastructure tooling, QA frameworks, "several years required". Below the threshold, nothing gets written. Above it, the CV is tailored, a short message drafted, and the package logged.
Result
Running for all four. Hours a week became a notification with a ready package attached — plus a log of what was skipped and why, so the filter can be argued with instead of trusted blindly.

A content pipeline that cannot publish an unverified claim

Livello GmbH n8nClaudeGPTMulti-agent~100 nodes

Problem
We were building a product comparison site where the content was partly AI-generated. I had met every requirement on the list and showed it to management.
The feedback
One question came back: is every single number on this page traceable to a source? It wasn't. The requirements had not asked for it, and I had not thought of it myself. That was the real gap.
Rebuild
I rebuilt the pipeline around verification instead of bolting it on. Research runs first, then a gap check that sends it back for anything missing. Specs are pulled into a ledger. Writing only starts once the numbers validate. After writing, an audit pass, then a separate manufacturer-verification pass, then a final QA — each able to route back into a fix loop. A publish gate sits at the end and blocks anything that has not cleared all of them; genuinely ambiguous cases stop and wait for a human.
Result
Management approved the rebuilt system with the governance logic. What I took from it: with generative AI, traceability is not a feature you add at the end — it belongs in the architecture from the first sketch.

Replacing paid lead generation with an agent that reacts to people

Livello GmbH n8nGPT-4o miniCRMEmail

Problem
Paid search was our lead engine, and cost per acquisition kept climbing as the segment got more competitive. So we asked what we were actually buying: conversations with the right decision makers.
Build
An agent researches companies against our criteria, identifies decision makers, enriches the profile, and drafts outreach with arguments that fit that industry. The part I care about is what happens next: the flow branches on how the person replies. A hard no stops the contact permanently. An out-of-office schedules itself past the return date. Genuine interest hands straight to a salesperson.
Result
Running cost dropped by roughly 95 percent against the campaigns it replaced, and effectiveness rose about 90 percent measured as qualified contacts per unit of effort.

A weekly briefing each department would actually read

Livello GmbH MakeCustom GPTResearch

Problem
Staying current was expected, so every department kept sending someone off to gather market and competitor news by hand and present it back to the team.
Approach
Before building anything I interviewed the lead of each department: what matters to you, which sources do you trust, what would you actually open. Those answers became the requirements — one system, a different definition of relevant per department.
Build
Research runs against the trusted sources, deduplicates against previous editions so nothing repeats, checks the sources resolve, and writes a recommendation aimed at that specific department. Every edition opens with a one-minute version for whoever has no time for the full thing.
Result
Ships twice a week, tailored per department, in the company's own layout. The manual research round disappeared.
